BThis page is a Deployment

The landing page is itself running on the cluster. The terminal deletes the page's own sections, they shatter, and Kubernetes rebuilds them through Pending, then ContainerCreating, then Running. It's the kind of thing people screen-record. Palette: Highlighter (8).

CScale to a thousand

One command, and a thousand Pods pour out of the scheduler into ten nodes while a giant counter runs up. Then a node is drained and its Pods move, then everything scales back down to three. The scale is the spectacle. Palette: Control plane (13).

DPods you can pick up

Pods are physical objects. Drag them and throw them, or click a node's name to drain it: its floor opens, the Pods fall out and the scheduler lifts each one into another node. Palette: Paper and ink (2).
