# Code of conduct

kubesim follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, and remember that everyone here is learning something.

Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't accepted in issues, pull requests, or anywhere else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, email the maintainer at sharathchenna87@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and ban people who keep breaking these rules, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
