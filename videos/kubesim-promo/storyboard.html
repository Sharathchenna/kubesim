kubesim · “Kubernetes you can see” storyboard v1 · sketches

A 45s square promo for X that teaches three real Kubernetes behaviours on an isometric kubesim cluster. No voiceover; the on-screen lines carry it.

1080×1080 · ~45s · 7 frames · music + SFX
Act 1 · the pain and the promise (0–9.5s) · Act 2 · three lessons (9.5–35s) · Act 3 · proof and ask (35–45s)
01 · JARGON WALLframe-01 · 0.0–4.5s

Words cycle in one slot first ("Pods?" → "ReplicaSets?" → "Controllers?" → "Schedulers?", hard cuts ~0.5s each, getting faster). The hero line slams in and blasts the jargon outward as chips. Shown: the moment after impact.

seam in: cut
02 · A CLUSTER YOU CAN SEEframe-02 · 4.5–9.5s

The hero line collapses to a dot that unfolds into the grid. The four Node slabs rise from below in a stagger (control-plane first, then the workers) and the system components click on in violet. A first Pod drops, then the kubesim lockup springs in above.

seam in: zoom-through
03 · SELF-HEALINGframe-03 · 9.5–19.0s

Three blue Pods drop onto the three workers ("Want 3 Pods."). Then "Kill one.": the Pod on worker-3 shatters into voxels and the readout flips to actual 2 in red. The ReplicaSet ring pulses and fires a beam, and a new Pod pops into the dashed slot ("Kubernetes brings it back."). The camera orbits slowly the whole time.

seam in: crossfade
04 · SCALINGframe-04 · 19.0–26.0s

The terminal pill slides in and types the command ("Need more?" → "Scale to 6."). On Enter, three new Pods rain down with motion trails, squash on landing and settle one per worker. The caption lands last.

seam in: push-slide LEFT
05 · A NODE DIESframe-05 · 26.0–35.0s

worker-3 glitches with an RGB-split flicker, then turns red, cracks and sinks. Its Pods turn grey (Unknown) and lift off, then arc onto the dashed slots on healthy workers and turn blue again. The chips step NotReady → Evicted → Running.

seam in: cut
06 · WHAT'S INSIDEframe-06 · 35.0–40.0s

The real Sandbox window floats up with a soft 3D tilt that settles flat, then the benefit pills pop around it one by one (~0.5s apart). This is the only real UI in the film. It's truthful: the isometric look is stylised, and this is the actual app.

seam in: blur-crossfade
07 · TRY ITframe-07 · 40.0–45.0s

The mark builds from a single Pod cube that morphs into the hexagon, and the wordmark types beside it. The terminal pill types the joke command and the output prints green. Hold still for the last 1.5s (the held frame).

seam in: zoom-through

Type

  • Display: Geist 600, −3% tracking (captions 60–120px)
  • Body: Geist 500 in muted slate
  • Mono: Geist Mono (kubectl, labels, chips)
  • Source: the app's layout.tsx / globals.css

Bans

  • No neon, glow or dark mode (the user chose light)
  • No text over busy motion; effects go around the copy
  • No slideshow: the cluster persists 02→05 as the hero prop
  • No screensaver: every move is a Kubernetes event
  • No fake app UI: frame 06 uses the real screenshot
