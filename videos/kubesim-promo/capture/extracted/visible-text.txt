[h3] Guided scenarios
[p] Each scenario runs real Kubernetes behaviour on the simulated cluster. Watch the canvas and the command log.
[span] Deploy & load-balance
[span] Create a 3-replica Deployment, expose it, and send traffic through the Service.
[span] Self-healing
[span] Delete a Pod and watch the ReplicaSet replace it within a second.
[span] Rolling update
[span] Change the image. New Pods (new colour) come up one at a time before old ones leave.
[span] Broken rollout → undo
[span] Roll out a typo'd image. It stalls in ImagePullBackOff while old Pods keep serving. Then undo.
[span] CrashLoopBackOff
[span] A Pod whose process exits with code 1. Watch restarts back off: 10s, 20s, 40s…
[span] Node failure
[span] Power off a worker. NotReady after ~50s, eviction after 5 more minutes. Runs at 30×.
[span] Drain for maintenance
[span] Cordon and drain a worker: its Pods move elsewhere, gracefully, with no downtime.
