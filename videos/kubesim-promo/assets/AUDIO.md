# Audio

The music and sound effects used by this promo are not in the repository. They
came from the HeyGen audio library (music: "Astral Generated Music f328b92b";
SFX from the HeyGen sound-effects library), whose terms govern their use and do
not allow redistributing them under this repo's MIT license.

To render the promo, put your own files at the paths `audio_meta.json` expects:

- `assets/bgm/track.mp3` (the background track, about 42 s)
- `assets/sfx/*.mp3` (the effects named in `audio_meta.json`)
